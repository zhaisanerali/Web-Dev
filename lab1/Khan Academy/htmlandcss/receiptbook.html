Spaghetti Bolognese

  • Time: 40 minutes
  • Serves: 2–3 people
Ingredients Quantity
Spaghetti 200 g
Minced beef 250 g
Tomato sauce 200 ml
Onion 1
Garlic 2 cloves
Olive oil 2 tbsp
Salt, pepper To taste

Step 1: Boil spaghetti in salted water until soft.

Step 2: Chop onion and garlic, fry them in olive oil.

Step 3: Add minced beef and cook until brown.

Step 4: Pour in tomato sauce, add salt and pepper.

Step 5: Simmer for 15 minutes and serve with spaghetti.

Chicken Caesar Salad

  • Time: 20 minutes
  • Serves: 2 people
Ingredients Quantity
Chicken breast 200 g
Lettuce 1 bunch
Croutons 50 g
Parmesan cheese 30 g
Caesar dressing 3 tbsp
Olive oil 1 tbsp

Step 1: Grill or fry the chicken and cut into slices.

Step 2: Wash and chop the lettuce.

Step 3: Add chicken, croutons, and cheese.

Step 4: Pour dressing on top and mix gently.

Pancakes

  • Time: 15 minutes
  • Serves: 3-4 people
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 1 cup
Milk 1 cup
Egg 1
Sugar 2 tbsp
Baking powder 1 tsp
Oil 1 tbsp

Step 1: Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder.

Step 2: Add milk and egg, whisk until smooth

Step 3: Heat a pan and add a little oil..

Step 4: Fry pancakes until golden on both sides.
